# Misty Widget Studio 4.6.4 — conservative repair

Source: complete **v4.6.1** GitHub release, not v4.6.2 or v4.6.3.

## What is fixed
- The previous patches changed `ThemePalette.paint()` without actually binding every outer panel to the configured background swatch. That left many widgets appearing unchanged.
- Now **only outer panel** backgrounds call a shared `.panel()` function. With `Theme colours → Custom theme` turned on, the same `Background` colour and `Style & layout → Background opacity` produce the same source RGBA for panels across widgets.
- Children (weather planet, CPU/GPU cards, terminal letters, pet sprites, fish, text) retain their original colours and opacity. This avoids the Weather / Planet visual regression.
- Liquid Glass in custom-colour mode uses a **single flat panel** rather than repeatedly tinting its gradient. Original silver glass remains available when custom colours are off.
- No changes to the original weather images, weather fetch script, SVG assets or CDN endpoints; no additional scripts or dependencies.
- The Eye of Cthulhu pet has been removed from the menu, stored selection fallback, sprites and terminal artwork. The remaining pets are **penguin, gecko and slime**.

## Important conditions
- You must select **Theme colours → Use my colours**, choose a preset or fill Background, and press **Apply**. The style Accent field is not the same as the background colour field.
- On KDE, transparency shows the wallpaper behind a widget. The same RGBA at different desktop positions may look different because the wallpapers differ.
- Optional `Weather → Transparent background` overrides panel fill on that one widget; disable it if matching other panels.
- Plant/Moon are intentionally floating, no panel by default, even if their colour presets are selected.
- Not yet rendered on a running KDE Plasma 6 installation. QML runtime verification remains needed.

## Rollback
Use `bash restore.sh` from the extracted package, then `systemctl --user restart plasma-plasmashell.service`.
